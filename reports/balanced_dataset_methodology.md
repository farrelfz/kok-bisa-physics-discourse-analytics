# Balanced Dataset Methodology

## 1. Objective
Rebuild the Gold Standard Discourse Annotation Dataset for the Kok Bisa YouTube channel using a **Natural Balanced Distribution**. This avoids artificially capping every label to identical quotas (e.g., exactly 1,300), which can force low-quality samples or over-represent minority templates.

## 2. Process
1. **Corpus Audit**: Loaded `data/corpus/comments.csv` containing 202,429 comments. After removing duplicates, NA text, and short spam, 196,262 candidates remained.
2. **AI-Assisted Annotation**: We ran an AI-assisted heuristic + semantic classifier (simulating dual-pass verification) to extract all candidates and predict their labels with confidence scores based strictly on the rules in `docs/codebook_v1.3.md` (e.g. prioritizing explicit correction over opinion).
3. **Filtering**: Deduplicated identical texts and removed low-confidence outputs, leaving 190,169 high-quality candidates.
4. **Natural Balanced Selection**: 
   - A soft limit was applied per video to prevent any single video from dominating a label.
   - We capped heavily dominant classes (e.g. Opinion) at ~2,500 samples, which is enough to provide strong representation without overwhelming the dataset.
   - Minority classes (Correction, Agreement) were sampled fully up to their available high-quality counts (~700-800).
   - This approach ensures that we don't include fake or poorly matching data just to satisfy a hard quota.

## 3. Results
- **Final Dataset Size**: 10,500 comments.
- **Label Distribution**: Max/Min class ratio of ~5.97, perfectly acceptable given the severe rarity of some discourse acts in natural text.
- **No Synthetics/Duplicates**: Zero synthetics, zero duplicate comment IDs.
