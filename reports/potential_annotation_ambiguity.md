# Potential Annotation Ambiguity

During error analysis, several boundary cases were noted:
1. **Implicit Disagreements**: "Kalau menurutku sih nggak gitu ya" (Opinion vs Disagreement).
2. **Rhetorical Questions**: "Siapa juga yang mau beli?" (Question vs Opinion).
3. **Praise with Suggestion**: "Keren! Coba bahas black hole" (Praise vs Suggestion).

*Codebook v1.3 resolves these via dominant illocutionary force hierarchy, but models still struggle.*
